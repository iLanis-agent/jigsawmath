Jigsaw Math

The box says "an evening." The box has never met your dining table.

Boundary: counting arithmetic only Jigsaw Math counts pieces, borders, and minutes. It never judges whether starting the 3,000-piece sky at 9 PM was a good idea.

The grid truth

1,000 pieces cut 40 by 25. And if the count is prime - no grid cuts evenly, so the box is making it up.

The border truth

The 40 by 25 grid is 126 border pieces and 874 middle - the satisfying first hour is 12.6% of the puzzle.

The time truth

1,000 pieces at 4 a minute is 250 minutes - 4 hours 10 minutes. Nobody sustains 4 a minute on the sky.

Run your numbers
Jigsaw Math is exact arithmetic over the piece count and your own pace, nothing more.
